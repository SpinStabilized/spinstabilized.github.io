## Rigs

### Home Shack

In the home shack we usually are operating on the following two radios:

For HF, we run an [Icom IC-7300](https://www.dxengineering.com/parts/ico-ic-7300) currently connected to a [Spooltenna](https://www.spooltenna.com) EFHW but I'm planning to swap it out with my kit-built [ARRL EFHW](https://home.arrl.org/action/Store/Product-Details/productId/133267?page=1) and moving the spooltenna to my portable kit.

For VHF/UFH we run an [Icom IC-2200H](https://www.rigpix.com/icom/ic2200h.htm) into a [SignalStuff OSJ](https://signalstuff.com/products/ss-osj/). I'm hoping to upgrade that rig for voice communications and dedicate the IC-2200H to be a APRS digipeater soon.

### Portable

For portable operations, we operate with a [Yaesu FT-991A](https://www.dxengineering.com/parts/ysu-ft-991a) with a [Super Antenna MP1DXMAX](https://www.hamradio.com/detail.cfm?pid=H0-015940) or a [4wave antennas vertical](https://4waveantennas.com/). Planning to add in a QRP rig at some point in the future for when the portable needs to be even more portable.
